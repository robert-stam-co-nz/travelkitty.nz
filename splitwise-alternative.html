A Splitwise alternative for group trips

Travel Kitty · checked against Splitwise’s own pages on 15 November 2026

Splitwise is great for flatmates and everyday bills. On a trip, its free plan gets in the way. Each person can add four expenses a day, there are ads, and converting currencies is part of Pro. If you’re the one keeping track, four is breakfast, a train, lunch and a museum, and dinner still has to go somewhere.

We built Travel Kitty for our own trips. It’s a shared travel expense app for Android: set a daily budget, log what everyone spends and see who owes what, any time.

Free to start. No card, no trial, no ads.

Made for trips

  • A daily budget. Set one for the trip and your balance shows how far ahead of or behind it you are, every day.
  • A map of where the money went. Pin each expense, then get directions back there in Google Maps.
  • Top-ups and refunds. Add money to the kitty, or record money that came back, and the balance stays right.
  • Trips you can archive. When a trip’s over, archive it and start the next. It’s still there to look back on, and archived trips don’t count toward your one Free trip.
  • New Zealand bank import. You can bring in your own transactions through Akahu. It takes a little setting up: you make a personal app with Akahu, then add its tokens under Settings → Advanced. On Free, transactions arrive in Uncategorised for you to sort.

Where Splitwise is the better choice

We’d rather you picked the app that suits you.

  • Everyone on iPhone. Travel Kitty is an Android app. Friends on an iPhone can follow the trip and see who owes what in their browser, but adding an expense needs the Android app. Splitwise has apps for iPhone, Android and the web.
  • Uneven splits. Travel Kitty splits each expense evenly between the people you pick. If one person owes more, you enter two expenses. Splitwise can split by percentages or shares.
  • Flatmates and ongoing bills. Splitwise has recurring expenses and isn’t tied to a trip. Travel Kitty is built around one trip at a time.
  • A currency we don’t have. Splitwise lists more than 100 currencies. Travel Kitty has 29.

Questions

Is Travel Kitty free?

Yes. Free has no ads and no daily limit, and you can run one trip of your own at a time with up to 20 people on it, counting you. Trips you’re invited to don’t count, so join as many as you like. Premium adds unlimited trips, 300 receipt scans a month, photo backup, exports and your own categories. In New Zealand it’s NZ$4.99 a month or NZ$49.99 a year, and Google Play shows the price where you are.

Can my friends on iPhone join?

Yes. Add their email address to the trip and Travel Kitty gives you a message to send them. They sign in with that address and follow every expense in their browser, with nothing to install. To add or change an expense, they’ll need someone with the Android app.

Does it work overseas with no signal?

Mostly, yes. Expenses save straight to your phone and sync when you’re back online. Looking up a new exchange rate needs a connection. Without one, Travel Kitty uses the last rate it has, or saves the expense without a rate for you to convert later.
