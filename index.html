What it is

One shared kitty for the whole trip

Set a daily budget, log what you spend, and let Travel Kitty do the maths.

Every expense, split for you Scan a receipt, attach a photo, drop a pin, and log each expense in any of 152 currencies. Travel Kitty splits it between everyone who shared it, automatically.
Keeps working with no signal Expenses save straight to your phone and sync the moment you’re back online. A few things need a connection, like a new exchange rate or a receipt scan.
Friends on iPhone can follow along Add their email address and Travel Kitty gives you a message to send them. They can follow every expense and see who owes what in their browser, with nothing to install.
No ads, no trackers No analytics either. And with Premium, you can export any trip to a spreadsheet.

Free and Premium

Most features are free

A few things cost us money to run: backing up photos, sorting bank transactions into categories and finding places by name. Those are Premium. Reading receipts costs us too, so Free includes three scans and Premium gives you 300 a month. Premium also has unlimited trips, exports and your own categories.

Free includes charts, settling up, maps, exchange rates, bank imports you sort yourself, and dark mode. No ads, no trackers, on either plan.

Free

NZ$0. No card, no trial, no ads.

Everything you need to keep track of shared travel expenses, without spending a cent.

Run the trip

  • One active trip, unlimited archivedRun one trip of your own at a time. When it’s over, archive it to start the next. Archived trips are still there to look back on, and trips you’re invited to don’t count, so join as many as you like.
  • Up to 20 people per tripAny trip can have up to 20 people on it, counting you. That’s the same on both plans.
  • Spend in 152 currenciesTravel Kitty looks up the day’s exchange rate and locks it in, so an expense keeps that value unless you change its currency or the trip’s.
  • Three free receipt scansTake a photo of a receipt and Travel Kitty fills in the amount, currency, date, shop and category for you to check before you save. On Free, the receipt stays on your phone.

Know where everyone stands

  • Charts, settling up, top-ups and mapsSee where the money goes in Insights, and get the fewest payments that settle everyone up. Keep track of top-ups and refunds. Pin your expenses on the map, with directions back to each place.
  • Import transactions from your bankBank in New Zealand? You can bring in your own transactions through Akahu. It takes a little setting up: you make a personal app with Akahu, then add its tokens under Settings → Advanced. On Free, transactions arrive in Uncategorised for you to sort.
  • Categories and profile photosKeep expenses organised with built-in categories, and add a profile photo so everyone knows who’s who.
  • Edit expenses in bulkNeed to change a bunch of categories or delete several expenses? Filter the list and make those changes all at once.
  • Make each trip your ownAdd a cover photo, and everyone on the trip sees it.
  • Keep your travel memoriesTake a photo of the places where you spend, and look back on them whenever you like. On Free, place photos stay on your phone, and the others on your trip can’t see them.
  • Mostly works offline, in light or darkYour expenses save on your phone and sync when you’re back online. A new exchange rate or a receipt scan needs a connection. And Travel Kitty comes in light and dark, whichever you prefer.

Premium

NZ$4.99 a month or NZ$49.99 a year

Everything in Free, plus

  • Unlimited tripsHave as many trips of your own on the go as you like. Everything you’ve already entered stays where it is.
  • Your photos, backed upYour place photos are backed up online and shared with everyone on the trip. Get a new phone? They come with you.
  • Scan 300 receipts a monthKeep scanning after your first three. Each receipt is backed up with its expense, so it comes with you to a new phone. Only you can see it.
  • Find places by namePaying for lunch and want to pin the place? Just type its name. Travel Kitty suggests places nearby and drops the pin for you. After that, Directions opens the place’s own page in Google Maps.
  • Bank transactions sorted for youImport from your bank and Travel Kitty sorts your transactions into categories by shop name, so there’s less for you to do. Anything it can’t place goes in Uncategorised rather than a guess.
  • Export your expensesExport any trip’s expenses as a CSV file you can open in any spreadsheet.
  • Make your own categoriesEveryone tracks spending a little differently. Make your own categories to suit the way you travel.

No ads. No trackers. No analytics. On both plans.
