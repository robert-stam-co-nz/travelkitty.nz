What it is

One shared kitty for the whole trip

Set a daily budget. Log what you spend as you go. Travel Kitty does the maths.

Each expense can carry a photo, a place on the map and its own currency, and it splits across whoever shared it.

It works with no signal. Expenses save straight away and sync later, which is the point on a real trip.

Free and Premium

Nearly all of it is free

Two things cost real money to run: storing your photos, and reading your bank statements for you. Those are Premium.

Everything else is free. Charts, settling up, the map, exchange rates, dark mode. No ads and no trackers, on either plan.

Free

NZ$0. No card, no trial, no ads.

  • Two trips of your ownTrips other people invite you to do not count. Join as many as you like.
  • Up to 20 people on a tripThe same on both plans. Your plan never limits anyone you invite.
  • Any currency, converted for youTravel Kitty looks up the day’s rate and sticks to it, so an expense never changes price later.
  • Charts, settle-up, surplus and the mapAll of Insights. The shortest way to square up. Manage surplus. Places pinned on the map.
  • Your own categories and a profile photoBuild the list of categories you actually use, and put a face to your name.
  • Filter and bulk-edit expensesChange the category on a whole run of rows, or delete them, in one go.
  • A cover photo on every tripOne per trip, shared with everyone on it.
  • Photos of where you spentTake them and look at them. On Free they stay on your phone, and nobody else on the trip sees them.
  • CSV export, alwaysYour data is yours. You never have to pay to get it out.
  • Works offline, and in the darkExpenses save with no signal and sync later. Light and dark themes throughout.

Premium

NZ$4.99 a month, or NZ$39.99 a year

  • Everything in FreeNothing is taken away, and anything you have already entered stays yours.
  • As many trips as you likeThe limit of two goes away.
  • Photos stored and sharedYour place photos upload. Everyone on the trip can see them, and they come back on a new phone.
  • Statements sorted for youImport a CSV, CAMT, OFX or QIF file. Travel Kitty reads the shop names and sorts the rows into categories.
  • Connect a bankLink up to three banks and pull in new transactions whenever you want, with no file to download. Wise, Revolut and about 9,700 others.In the test build this runs against Plaid’s test system. Use the sandbox login, not your real bank.
  • Email support within a business dayA reply from the person who built it.

Testers pay nothing. Everyone in the group is on Google’s licence testing list, so Premium is free while the test runs and you will never be charged. If Play does ask you for money, stop and tell me. That means something is wrong at my end.

No ads. No trackers. No analytics. On both plans.

Come and break it

Be in the first group to use it

Travel Kitty is in closed testing. A small group are using it on real trips and telling me what is wrong with it. That group is open, and it is where the app gets decided.

Be in first Get it before it reaches the Play Store properly. Every paid feature is unlocked and there is nothing to pay.
Ask for what is missing Most of what is in the app now is there because somebody asked for it. Say what you want and there is a good chance you will see it.
Be part of the journey Watch it go from a closed test to a real release, and know which bits are there because of you.
Ask to join the group
  1. Ask to join the group, on your phone if you can. Google signs you in before it lets you ask, and that is how I get the right account. On a computer, check you are signed in as the account your phone uses. Settings › Google on the phone will tell you which one that is.
  2. I approve you, then send the opt-in link. Open it on your phone and it takes you to the Play Store, where you install as normal. I approve by hand, so give me a day.
  3. Tell me what confused you, by posting in the group. It comes straight to me and no other tester sees it. Bugs turn up on their own. The moment you did not know what to do next is the thing I cannot see from here.
