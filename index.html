Free and Premium

Nearly all of it is free

Storing your photos and sorting your bank transactions cost real money to run, so those are Premium. So are unlimited trips, exporting your expenses and making your own categories.

Everything else is free: charts, settling up, the map, exchange rates, bank import, dark mode. No ads, no trackers, on either plan.

Free

NZ$0. No card, no trial, no ads. Just get started.

Everything you need to keep track of shared travel expenses, without spending a cent.

Your trips, your way

  • Two trips of your ownCreate up to two trips of your own, for free. Trips someone else invites you to don’t count towards your limit, so you can join as many as you like.
  • Up to 20 people per tripTravelling with friends, family or a group? Invite up to 20 people to any trip. Your plan won’t limit the people you can bring along.
  • Every currency, taken care ofSpending in another currency? No worries. Travel Kitty looks up the day’s exchange rate and locks it in, so an expense never changes value later.

Everything you need to settle up

  • Charts, settle-ups, top-ups and mapsSee where your money goes with Insights, find the easiest way to settle up, keep track of top-ups and refunds, and pin the places you’ve spent money on the map.
  • Import transactions from your bankConnect your NZ bank through Akahu or bring in your Wise transactions. They’ll arrive in Uncategorised, ready for you to sort through.
  • Categories and your own profile photoKeep expenses organised with built-in categories, and add a profile photo so everyone knows who’s who.
  • Filter and edit expenses in bulkNeed to change a bunch of categories or delete several expenses? Filter the list and make those changes all at once.
  • Give every trip its own cover photoAdd a cover photo to each trip to make it feel like yours. Everyone on the trip gets to see it.
  • Keep photos of the places you’ve beenTake photos of the places where you spent money and look back on them whenever you like. On Free, they’re stored on your phone and aren’t shared with the rest of your trip.
  • Ready when you are, even offlineNo signal? No problem. Your expenses save on your phone and sync when you’re back online. And whether you prefer light or dark, Travel Kitty has you covered.

Premium

NZ$4.99 a month or NZ$39.99 a year

For those who want a little more room to travel, keep everything organised and spend less time sorting out expenses.

Everything in Free, plus a little extra

  • As many trips as you likeTwo trips aren’t always enough. With Premium, create as many of your own trips as you need. Everything you’ve already entered stays right where it is.
  • Your photos, wherever you goYour place photos are stored and shared with everyone on the trip. Get a new phone? Your photos come with you.
  • Bank transactions sorted for youImport from your bank and let Travel Kitty do the busywork. It reads the shop names and automatically puts transactions into the right categories, so there’s less sorting for you to do.
  • Export your expensesWant a copy of everything? Download any trip’s expenses as a CSV spreadsheet, ready to open and use wherever you need.
  • Make categories your ownEveryone tracks spending a little differently. Create your own categories to suit the way you travel and the things you spend money on.
  • Real help from a real personGot a question or need a hand? You’ll get a reply from the person who built Travel Kitty within one business day.
