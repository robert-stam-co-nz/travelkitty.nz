Delete your Travel Kitty data

Travel Kitty · last updated 23 November 2026

You don’t have to delete your account to delete your data. Inside the app, you can remove a single expense or a whole trip on its own, and the photos attached to them go at the same time.

Deleting your account removes everything at once. If you can’t sign in, you can ask us to do it by email.

Delete a single expense

  1. Open the trip and go to the Expenses tab.
  2. Tap the expense to open it.
  3. Tap the bin icon in the top bar.
  4. Confirm Delete.

The expense is removed for everyone on the trip, and the balances update to match. Its photo is removed from the expense, and on Premium from our storage too, even one that was taken offline and hadn’t finished uploading yet. The copy in your phone’s Travel Kitty album stays. A receipt you scanned for it goes too.

Delete a whole trip

  1. Open the trip.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the trip header.
  3. Tap Delete trip.
  4. Travel Kitty tells you what will go: the trip’s expenses, their photos, the balances and every invite link. Type the trip’s name to confirm.

Everything that belongs to the trip goes: every expense and its photo, the trip’s cover photo, the list of travellers and the trip’s invite links.

Only the trip’s owner can delete it, and deleting it removes it for everyone on it. If you’re on a trip but don’t own it, you can leave it instead: swipe it sideways on the trips list and confirm Leave. Leaving takes you off the list of travellers. The expenses you added stay, because the balances wouldn’t add up without them.

Deleting an expense or a trip happens straight away and can’t be undone. If you want a record first, on Premium you can export the trip from Settings before you delete it.

Delete your account, in the app

  1. Open Travel Kitty.
  2. Tap your avatar, top right, to open Settings.
  3. Tap your name to open Profile.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  5. Travel Kitty tells you how many trips will be deleted and how many you’ll leave. Type DELETE to confirm. Then confirm it’s you with your password, or with Google if that’s how you sign in.

Deletion starts straight away and can’t be undone.

Delete your account, by email

If you can’t sign in, email support@travelkitty.nz from the email address your account uses, with the subject Delete my account. We’ll do it within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Sending from your account’s own address is how we know the request is yours. We can’t act on a request from a different address, because we’d have no way to tell it apart from someone trying to delete a stranger’s data.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Premium subscription. It renews, and Google charges you for it, until you cancel it yourself: Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions. Nothing on this page and nothing in the app can do it for you: the agreement is between you and Google. Cancel there as well, in whichever order you like, or you’ll keep paying for an app you’ve deleted.

If you own a trip that other people are still using, deleting your account deletes it for them too. If you want the trip to carry on, ask someone else on it to set it up again, or export it first from Settings (Premium) before you delete.

How long it takes

Deleting in the app happens straight away. Travel Kitty works through your trips, removes their photos, and then removes the account itself. Backups held by our hosting provider roll off within 30 days.

Nothing is kept for marketing, and there’s no grace period during which the account can be restored.
