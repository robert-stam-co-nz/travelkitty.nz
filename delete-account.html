Delete your Travel Kitty account

Travel Kitty · last updated 1 September 2026

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app, or by asking by email if you cannot get in.

In the app

  1. Open Travel Kitty.
  2. Tap your avatar, top right, to open Settings.
  3. Tap your name to open Profile.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  5. The app tells you how many trips will be deleted and how many you will leave. Type DELETE to confirm, then enter your password.

Deletion begins immediately and cannot be undone.

By email

If you cannot sign in, send a message to robert.stam@outlook.co.nz from the email address the account uses, with the subject Delete my account. The request will be actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner. Sending from the account's own address is what confirms the request is yours; a request from a different address cannot be honoured, because there would be no way to tell it apart from someone trying to delete a stranger's data.

What is deleted

DeletedKept
Your profile — display name, avatar colour
Your sign-in credentials
Your custom categories
Every trip you own, for everyone on it
All expenses, photos and balances on those trips
Invitations you sent 		Trips owned by someone else, which you are simply removed from
Expenses you added to those trips, because the balances would not add up without them — they show as added by a former member

If you own a trip that other people are still using, deleting your account deletes it for them too. If you want the trip to survive, ask another member to recreate it, or export it first from Settings → Import & export data before you delete.

How long it takes

An in-app deletion is immediate: the app works through your trips, removes their photos, and then removes the account itself. Backups held by the hosting provider roll off within 30 days. Nothing is retained for marketing, and there is no grace period during which the account can be restored.
