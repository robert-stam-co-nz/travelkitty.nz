Delete your Travel Kitty data

Travel Kitty · last updated 4 September 2026

You do not have to delete your account to delete your data. A single expense and a whole trip can each be removed on their own, from inside the app, and the photos attached to them go at the same time. Deleting the account removes everything at once, and can also be requested by email if you cannot sign in.

Delete a single expense

  1. Open the trip and go to the Expenses tab.
  2. Tap the expense to open it.
  3. Tap the bin icon in the top bar.
  4. Confirm Delete.

The expense is removed for everyone on the trip and the balances update to match. Its receipt photo is deleted from storage with it — including one that was taken offline and had not finished uploading yet.

Delete a whole trip

  1. Open the trip.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the trip header.
  3. Tap Delete trip.
  4. The app tells you how many expenses will go. Confirm to delete.

Everything belonging to the trip goes: every expense and its photo, the trip's cover photo, the member list, the invite code and any unanswered invitations. Only the owner of a trip can delete it, and deleting it removes it for everyone on it. If you are a member rather than the owner, you can leave the trip instead: swipe it sideways on the trips list and confirm Leave. Leaving removes you from the member list; the expenses you added stay, because the balances would not add up without them.

Deleting an expense or a trip is immediate and cannot be undone. If you want a record first, export the trip from Settings → Import & export data before you delete it.

Delete your account, in the app

  1. Open Travel Kitty.
  2. Tap your avatar, top right, to open Settings.
  3. Tap your name to open Profile.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  5. The app tells you how many trips will be deleted and how many you will leave. Type DELETE to confirm, then enter your password.

Deletion begins immediately and cannot be undone.

Delete your account, by email

If you cannot sign in, send a message to support@travelkitty.nz from the email address the account uses, with the subject Delete my account. The request will be actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner. Sending from the account's own address is what confirms the request is yours; a request from a different address cannot be honoured, because there would be no way to tell it apart from someone trying to delete a stranger's data.

If you own a trip that other people are still using, deleting your account deletes it for them too. If you want the trip to survive, ask another member to recreate it, or export it first from Settings → Import & export data before you delete.

How long it takes

An in-app deletion is immediate: the app works through your trips, removes their photos, and then removes the account itself. Backups held by the hosting provider roll off within 30 days. Nothing is retained for marketing, and there is no grace period during which the account can be restored.
